About Us

Our story, mission, and values – rooted in soya

Mission & Vision

We empower rural communities through sustainable soya farming and value addition. Our vision is a prosperous Malawi where every soya farmer thrives.

  • Transparency – open books, open doors
  • Sustainability – eco‑friendly farming and processing
  • Empowerment – training, ownership, dignity

Founded in 2018, Nthakayathu started with 50 soya farmers. Today, we process over 200 tonnes of soya annually, producing premium oil, animal feed, and soya products.
