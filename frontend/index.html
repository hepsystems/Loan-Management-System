Mission & Vision

We empower rural communities through sustainable value addition. Our vision is a prosperous Malawi where every farmer thrives.

  • Transparency – open books, open doors
  • Sustainability – eco‑friendly and long‑term
  • Empowerment – training, ownership, dignity

Founded in 2018, Nthakayathu is a farmer‑owned cooperative that has grown from 50 to over 2,300 members.
