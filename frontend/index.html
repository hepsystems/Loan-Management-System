Mission & Vision

We empower rural communities through sustainable soya farming and value addition. Our vision is a prosperous Malawi where every soya farmer thrives.

  • Transparency – open books, open doors
  • Sustainability – eco‑friendly farming and processing
  • Empowerment – training, ownership, dignity

Founded in 2026, Nthakayathu started with 21 united soya farmers. Today, we process over 200 tonnes of soya annually, producing premium oil, animal feed, and soya products.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who we are

Nthakayathu Soya Cooperative ("we", "us", "the cooperative") operates this website to share information about our work, sell products, and manage our membership. This policy explains what information we collect through the site and how we use it.

2. Information we collect

When you register as a member, we collect your full name, username, email address, phone number (optional), the sub-committee you select (or your leadership position, if applicable), and a password. Your password is never stored in readable form — only a secure, one-way hash of it is kept, and we cannot see or recover your original password.

When you place an order, submit a project proposal, or use the contact form, we collect the details you choose to provide in that form (e.g. name, contact details, and message content).

3. How we use your information

We use member information to operate the cooperative — verifying who you are when you log in, showing your sub-committee/leadership position on the internal members roster, and letting cooperative admins manage membership (e.g. correcting a committee assignment, or blocking/removing an account if someone is no longer a member). We use order, proposal, and contact information to respond to your request.

4. Who can see your information

Only cooperative admins can view the full members list, and it is used solely for cooperative administration. The website admin (technical/site-management) account is separate from cooperative membership and does not automatically grant anyone cooperative office or committee status. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Registration by join code

To join as a member you need a join code issued by a cooperative admin. This helps ensure that only people known to the cooperative can register.

6. Data retention

We keep member records for as long as you remain a member, or until you ask us to remove your account (see below). Blocking an account keeps the record but prevents login; removing an account permanently deletes it.

7. Your choices

You can reset your password at any time using the "Forgot password?" link. To correct your details, update your committee/position, or permanently delete your account, contact a cooperative admin using the details on our Contact page.

8. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, please reach out via the Contact section of this website.
